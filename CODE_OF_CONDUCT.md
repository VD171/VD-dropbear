# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help the project:

- being respectful and welcoming;
- accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the users;
- showing empathy toward others.

Things that are not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private;
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces - the repository, issues, pull requests, and any
official channel listed in the README - and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **vd.priv8@pm.me**. Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban
contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
